Both algorithms were run on identical input: events [20250, 20750) of ColliderML ttbar pu0, with cells zero-suppressed at 0.0002 GeV. Target particles satisfy pT >= 0.5 GeV, |eta| <= 4.0, and at least 3 calorimeter cells counted after zero-suppression. Truth is the exclusive partition: each cell belongs to the particle depositing the most energy in it. Truth particles and predicted clusters are matched one-to-one by maximising shared calibrated energy with scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment; unmatched truth particles count as inefficiencies and unmatched clusters as fakes. Cell energies are calibrated per subsystem (ecb 37.5, ece 38.7, hcb 45.0, hce 46.9). Layer indices come from a frozen geometry of ecb 48, ece 48, hcb 36, hce 36 layers. MaskFormer results use checkpoint epoch=003-val_loss=18.06191.ckpt at mask threshold 0.5 and object threshold 0.2; it was trained on events [0, 20000], disjoint from those reported here. The model has two prediction heads and results are reported for both. The mask head scores each (query, cell) pair through an independent sigmoid; the incidence head applies a softmax over queries within a cell and is trained against the true energy fractions I_ia = E_ia / E_i. 'maskformer' resolves a contested cell to the query with the highest mask probability, 'maskformer_incidence' to the query holding the largest predicted share of that cell. Detection is the mask head's in both, so the two claim identical cells and differ only in assignment. A matched pair must share at least 0.05 of the smaller of the two energy totals, so a cluster merely grazing a particle is not counted as reconstructing it. Splitting and merging use a 0.1 threshold applied to calibrated energy rather than to cell counts. Uncertainties on fractions come from resampling events rather than particles, since particles within an event are not independent. Reported figures carry two reference clusterings: a geometric ceiling, in which an idealised method given the true particle count and shower axes assigns each cell to the nearest axis in angle and depth at the best of a scanned range of depth weightings; and a resolution ceiling, in which target particles sharing more than half of the smaller one's energy are merged and then clustered perfectly.
